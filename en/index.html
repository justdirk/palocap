Frequently asked questions

What we get
asked all the time

Can I bring my own garment?

Yes. Bring your own hoodie, jacket or shirt and we'll embroider whatever you like on it. Or choose one of ours.

What's the minimum order?

One piece. We do the single gift as well as a whole team's kit: the price per piece changes, the availability doesn't.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the garment, the technique and how many pieces. The quote is free and takes five minutes at the counter — or write to us and we'll reply with a price.

What file do I need for a logo?

Any image is fine to get the idea across. A vector file (AI, EPS, PDF) gives a cleaner result, especially for embroidery — but if you don't have one, we'll redraw it.
